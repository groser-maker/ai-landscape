# Ask Status Blocker / Context
1 Continued product parity — maintain 1-release-behind-GA cadence across all products 🟡 In Progress
Each product team manages parity independently with no cross-product enforcement mechanism. Progress is uneven — Field Service and Tableau Next on track; Revenue Cloud and Commerce at 0%. No centralized GPS-facing governance body to escalate to.
2 Sovereign Cloud roadmap — committed architecture path for EMEA and ANZ customers 🔴 Blocked
No committed architecture path or timeline from Product. EMEA South transition removed a distraction but didn't resolve underlying sovereign cloud gap. ANZ government funding reductions have muted urgency internally. GPS H2 ACV deals in EMEA and ANZ actively blocked waiting for a roadmap date customers can cite.
3 Industry AE investment — Health, Transportation, Tax & Revenue verticals 🟡 In Progress
Company-wide management hiring freeze limits near-term headcount additions. Budget requests for specialist AEs submitted but not yet approved. Industry clouds (Health, FSC) are gaining FedRAMP traction but lack dedicated AE coverage to capture the opportunity.
4 Move Informatica + Slack CS under GPS CS leader (as done in other OUs) ⚪ Not Started
No org decision made. Requires exec alignment between Informatica and Slack CS leadership and GPS. Other OUs have completed this transition — GPS is an outlier. Lack of unified CS structure creates gaps in renewal coverage and consumption activation for these products.
5 Hiring exception for key FLM roles — front-line manager headcount 🔴 Blocked
Company-wide management hiring freeze. Exception requests submitted; not yet approved at board level. GPS is at/above AE plan with surge headcount but FLM coverage is stretched. 113 RVPs managing an expanding remit without backfill approval.
6 GovCloud UIP development — accurate Agentforce + Data Cloud CCR / consumption visibility 🟡 In Progress
Engineering scoping underway. DISA authorization boundary requirements add complexity to telemetry pipelines — data cannot flow freely between GovCloud and commercial observability infrastructure. Without GovCloud UIP, GPS cannot accurately track which customers are consuming Agentforce/Data Cloud, blocking proactive CSM engagement.
7 ARI program funding — attrition avoidance support continuation 🟢 H1 Approved
ARI program delivered $29.3M in saved attrition in Q2 — highest performing OU. H1 funding approved. H2 continuation not yet formally committed — requires BLT sign-off. Without renewal, the GPS attrition rate (currently 7.6% TTM, improving) could reverse course in H2.
8 Executive engagement in Federal accounts — drive retention and ACV growth 🟡 In Progress
Senior Salesforce exec presence is a proven retention lever in federal — validated by VA ($194M TCV AELA) and Army HRC wins. No formal program structure; currently driven ad-hoc by RVPs. Continuing Resolution through Dec 11 is tightening agency budgets — executive-level engagement is critical to protect H2 commitments.
